Product

We monitor spindle bearings. And say what we do not monitor.

The spindle is the most expensive component of a machining centre, and bearing damage announces itself in the vibration pattern. That is what we focus on. Everything else is marked “in development”.

Available

Spindle and spindle bearings

A vibration sensor on the spindle housing measures what the CNC control cannot supply. The control's standard interfaces are not sufficient for bearing diagnostics because they sample too slowly.

Outer race damage

Assessed

Peaks at the ball pass frequency of the outer race (BPFO) and its harmonics in the envelope spectrum.

Inner race damage

Assessed

Ball pass frequency of the inner race (BPFI), typically with sidebands spaced at the rotational frequency.

Rolling element damage

Assessed

Characteristic frequency of the rolling elements (BSF). Harder to detect than race damage.

Cage damage

Assessed

Cage frequency (FTF), often an indication of lubrication problems or wear of the cage guidance.

Unbalance

Assessed

Increased vibration once per revolution, for example from a contaminated clamping device or a poorly balanced tool.

Assessment

Measured against your machine, not an average.

Every spindle vibrates differently, depending on age, installation and machining. That is why the system first learns the baseline of your spindle, i.e. its normal state. Only then are deviations meaningful.

Features instead of raw data

The edge box calculates features on site, such as RMS, crest factor, kurtosis and amplitudes at the bearing frequencies. Only these values are transmitted.

Trend instead of single value

A single outlier does not trigger an alarm. What matters is whether a feature drifts away from the baseline over hours and days.

Reasoning instead of traffic lights

Every alert names the feature, the suspected damage pattern and the size of the deviation. You give feedback on whether it was correct.

NormalWithin the baseline band.
NoticeableSlight deviation, being monitored.
WarningDamage pattern detected. Schedule an inspection.
AlarmClear deviation. Act promptly.

Limits

What we explicitly do not do.

  • Never write to the CNC control. No speed, feed or offset values, no parameter optimisation.
  • No prediction to the day. We show how far a spindle deviates from its baseline.
  • No promise of “zero downtime”. Nobody can guarantee that.
  • No operator or shift data. That spares you the consultation with your works council.
