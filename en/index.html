Condition monitoring for CNC spindles

A spindle bearing does not fail overnight. It gives warning.

Long before a bearing becomes audible, its vibration pattern changes. We measure that change on every spindle, compare it with the machine's learned baseline, i.e. the normal state of this spindle, and tell you what has changed. Across manufacturers and without writing to your CNC control.

How it works

Three steps. No intervention in your machine.

In plain words for managing directors and plant managers. The technical details are under Product.

  1. MeasureA vibration sensor sits on the spindle housing, a small edge box on the machine. The box reads from the CNC control how fast the spindle is turning. It changes nothing on your machine.
  2. CompareThe software learns how your healthy spindle vibrates and compares every new measurement with that. An incipient bearing defect changes the vibration pattern long before it becomes audible.
  3. AlertIf something deviates, your maintenance team receives an alert in plain language: which component is affected, how urgent it is and what to check.

Plan instead of react

Method

Explainable, not a black box.

Maintenance engineers will not stop a machine because an algorithm has a bad feeling. They want to know which value has changed and how. That is why every alert shows its reasoning.

  1. Measure

    A vibration sensor on the spindle housing, plus speed and, where available, load from the CNC control via OPC UA.

  2. Learn the baseline

    Every spindle vibrates differently. The system first learns how your machine sounds in a healthy state.

  3. Assess the deviation

    Features at the damage frequencies are checked against the learned baseline. What counts is the trend, not a single value.

  4. Alert with reasoning

    You see the feature, the damage pattern and the size of the deviation. With your feedback we calibrate the thresholds for your machine.

NormalWithin the baseline band.
NoticeableSlight deviation, being monitored.
WarningDamage pattern detected. Schedule an inspection.
AlarmClear deviation. Act promptly.

We deliberately do not predict failure to the day. Nobody can do that credibly.

Data security

Your IT can decide in ten minutes.

We document which data leaves your plant, over which port and in which direction. The edge box does not capture NC programs, drawings or operator data.

  • A
    No write functionThe software contains no code that writes to a CNC control.
  • B
    Upstream blockAny request that would change something is blocked before it reaches the CNC control.
  • C
    Automated checksTests ensure with every change that no write path is introduced.
  • D
    Read-only userA dedicated user with read rights only, for SINUMERIK for example SinuReadAll and AlarmRead.
