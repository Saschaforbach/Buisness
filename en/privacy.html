2. Collection and processing of personal data

We only collect personal data if you provide it to us voluntarily when contacting us or requesting an appointment. This includes in particular:

  • Name
  • Company
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Details of your machine's CNC control (optional)
  • Content of your request

Your data is not passed on to third parties unless this is necessary to perform a contract or you have expressly consented.

3. Contact form

If you send us a request via the contact form, we process your details solely to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and forward them to us by email. The transmission is encrypted (TLS) via a server of our German hosting provider ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich (owner René Münnich, Hauptstraße 68, 02742 Friedersdorf, Germany), with whom we have concluded a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. Your data is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for this purpose.

4. Hosting and server logs

This website is provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When our pages are accessed, server log files are created automatically, which may contain the following information: IP address, date and time of access, address accessed, browser type and version, and operating system. This data is used solely for the technical provision of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and is not combined with other data sources. Further information: GitHub Pages data collection.

5. Fonts and images

This website uses the fonts Archivo, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono. The font files are stored on our own web space and loaded from there. There is no connection to Google servers. All images are likewise loaded exclusively from our own web space. We do not use external script libraries or content delivery networks.

6. Local browser storage

Without your consent, this website does not use any tracking or analytics cookies. We only store your choice in the consent notice in your browser's local storage (entry “fp_cookie_consent”), so that the notice does not reappear on every visit. This entry contains no personal data and is not transmitted (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act).

7. LinkedIn Insight Tag (only with consent)

We use the LinkedIn Insight Tag of LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company (Wilton Plaza, Wilton Place, Dublin 2, Ireland). It is only loaded after you have expressly consented (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). If you decline, the tag is not loaded and no marketing cookies are set.

The Insight Tag helps us measure how successful our LinkedIn ads are, for example whether visitors send a request after clicking an ad. In doing so, a cookie is set and data such as IP address, device and browser information, page views and, where applicable, your LinkedIn member ID are transmitted to LinkedIn and partly transferred to the USA (basis: EU Standard Contractual Clauses). We only receive the analysis in aggregated, anonymous form.

You can withdraw your consent at any time via the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page. Further information: linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). To exercise these rights, contact info@forbachandpartners.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW).
