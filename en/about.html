About us

From manufacturing, not from software.

Twelve years of management consultancy in manufacturing companies. Only after that did we write the first line of code for condition monitoring.

Founder, managing director, software architect

Sascha P. Forbach

Too often I have seen a spindle run until it stops running. Then the machine stands still for days or weeks, and nobody can say when the damage first gave warning.

At 18 I began studying business administration alongside my job and completed a bachelor's degree. For twelve years I have worked as a management consultant with an unusually concrete focus: manufacturing processes, quality assurance and machine availability in manufacturing companies. Modern CNC controls have long supplied the data that announces a spindle failure. It simply is not analysed.

That is where our product came from: condition monitoring for mixed machine fleets of the kind found in every contract manufacturer (job shop). As software architect, I designed the edge box and the platform myself. They follow one rule: the machine is only ever read, never changed.

Conviction

German industry does not win on price. It wins when its machines run when they have to.

Manufacturers in Asia and Eastern Europe are cheaper, and that will not change. What they cannot copy so easily is reliable delivery at the highest precision. That is exactly what is lost when a spindle fails unexpectedly.

A German manufacturer that has its downtime under control delivers on time, holds tolerances and plans maintenance instead of suffering it. That is what we build our system for. Sascha P. Forbach

Mindset

I keep my own hands on the controls.

Wherever I am, I do not hand over responsibility. And for me, 100 per cent is only just good enough.

That is also how aviation thinks. Checks are made before every flight, not only once something fails. We build our system with the same attitude: a spindle announces its damage, and we make sure someone looks in time.

Principles

What you can hold us to.

In a market full of promises, what a supplier rules out says the most about it.

What we commit to

  • We never intervene in your CNC control, with four independent technical safeguards.
  • One view for all makes on your shop floor.
  • Every alert with explainable reasoning.
  • Data exclusively in data centres in Germany.
  • A clear answer in the introductory call on whether your CNC control can be connected.

What we do not promise

  • No “zero downtime”. Nobody can guarantee that.
  • No prediction to the day.
  • No optimisation of speed or feed. That would mean writing to your machine.
  • No figures from other people's projects presented as what you should expect.
