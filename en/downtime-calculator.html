Where to find the figures

Five values your controlling department knows.

If you do not know a value exactly, use a cautious estimate. The result will then tend to be too low rather than too high.

ValueWhere to find it
Cost per hour of downtimeMachine hourly rate from your costing. If orders wait or are lost, add the lost contribution margin per hour.
Unplanned downtime hoursMaintenance log, production data acquisition (MES) or machine logbook for the last two to three years. Count only downtime caused by the spindle and spindle bearings.
Number of unplanned spindle failuresInvoices from your spindle service provider and the machine manufacturer. An average over several years is more meaningful than a single year.
Extra cost per failureThe difference between an unplanned and a planned repair: rush surcharges, express shipping, consequential damage to spindle, tool and workpiece, extra shifts to catch up.
Avoidable shareYour own estimate. How many of the failures could you have moved into a maintenance window if you had known about them weeks in advance?
